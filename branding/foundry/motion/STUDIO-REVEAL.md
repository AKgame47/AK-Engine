# AK Engine / Studio Reveal

A 5.5-second title ident for review, based on the supplied red-and-white AK Engine artwork. This proposal does not change the engine, editor, exports, or existing Forge ident.

- [Finished MP4](AK-Engine-Studio-Reveal.mp4): 1920 × 1080, 24 fps, H.264 video, 48 kHz stereo AAC. Open the file and download it if GitHub does not show a player.
- [Editable Blender project](AK-Engine-Studio-Reveal.blend): editable machined-metal relief, camera, lighting, and compositor transition. The supplied logo and the synthesized sound are packed. Open with Blender 4.3.2 or compatible.

A softbox travels over the metal relief as the camera pulls back. At about 2.33 seconds it settles with a synchronized metal latch; by about 3.04 seconds the original flat artwork is fully visible. The finished MP4 applies extra denoising to the lit sequence.

Artwork supplied by AK Studio for this ident. Motion and sound produced for AK Studio. No external sound samples or film materials are included. This is an offline Blender asset, not a runtime dependency.

Verified: all 132 video frames decode, stereo audio is 48 kHz, and the packed source artwork matches the supplied PNG byte-for-byte.
